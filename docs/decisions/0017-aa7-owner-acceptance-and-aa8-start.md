# ADR-0017: Owner acceptance of Gate AA7 and start of AA8

## Accepted decision

On November 7, 2026, after disclosure of the final engineering results and 80 high worker-image advisories, the workspace owner instructed: “Close the gate, face AS7, and proceed to the other phase.” The owner immediately clarified: “aa7 phase”. This is explicit acceptance of Gate AA7 and authorization to proceed to Phase AA8. ADR-0009 assigns the workspace owner the product, privacy/security and evaluation approval roles.

Gate AA7 is CLOSED / OWNER ACCEPTED for the standalone Build converter on exact worker `sha256:4b83ffec76083b02a79df7addf5ff1f8e09b6d90135d73915b890b38b7753b16`, with the tested capabilities and disclosed limitations in the [final closeout](../evaluation/AA7_FREETYPE_FINAL_CLOSEOUT_20261107.md). This acceptance does not change scan results, test outcomes, thresholds or claim levels. Existing failed receipts remain intact. Document/assistive-technology certification and legal interpretation remain separately identified release obligations.

Production activation, environment promotion, deployment, publication and whole-project launch are deferred as previously directed by the owner. The approved default-off configuration stays unchanged. This decision closes the AA7 phase gate; it is not a statement that all vulnerabilities were remediated or every project release gate passed.
