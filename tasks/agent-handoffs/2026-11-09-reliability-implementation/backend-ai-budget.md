## Fixed interface

Exports are `createAiBudgetClient`, `calculateAiBudgetCost`, `hashAiBudgetBinding`, `AiBudgetError`, schema version `ai-budget/1.0.0` and their binding/usage/receipt/RPC types. The client exposes `reserve`, `claimDispatch`, `releaseBeforeDispatch`, `markUnknown`, `settle` and `reconcile`; each resolves the validated receipt directly, not its transport envelope.

Each binding includes server-resolved user/organization UUIDs, logical operation UUID, distinct attempt UUID, ordinal 1–3, surface qualitative/rewrite/build, payload SHA-256, model, prompt/policy versions and positive reserved input/output counts. The immutable operation hash excludes attempt UUID/ordinal; their receipt identities are checked independently. A fresh legitimate attempt does not alter the logical operation binding. The helper does not introduce automatic retries or pretend a same-attempt replay permits another dispatch.

RPC names are `ai_budget_reserve`, `ai_budget_claim_dispatch`, `ai_budget_release`, `ai_budget_mark_unknown`, `ai_budget_settle` and `ai_budget_reconcile`. Arguments are exactly `p_user_id`, `p_organization_id` and `p_request`. The base request has operationId, attemptId, attemptOrdinal, surface, payloadSha256, model, promptPolicyVersion, policyVersion, reservedInputTokens and reservedOutputTokens. Counts are canonical decimal strings on the wire. Settlement adds a strict known-usage pair; reconciliation also adds operator/reconciliation UUIDs and an evidence SHA. Caller period, prices, limits, opening amounts and arbitrary notes are rejected locally, not forwarded.

Strict versioned response envelopes are either ok plus receipt or denied plus one bounded error code. Missing/failed/throwing RPCs, malformed envelopes, unexpected fields, altered identities, invalid amounts, inconsistent costs or impossible lifecycle flags fail closed with safe `AiBudgetError`. No raw database error/cause is returned. The receipt binds owner/org/operation/attempt, ordinal/cap, original payload/model/prompt/attempt policy, the separate captured pool policy and database period, explicit rates, reserved/actual counters and replay/dispatch/block flags. Pool policy may differ from the per-operation rate policy; rotation must not reset capacity. The client validates period syntax without using its own clock to choose a month.

Only a fresh successful claim receipt may grant dispatch. Reserve and every replay cannot grant it. Released/held/settled results must match their requested command, and known settlement/reconciliation must match the exact supplied usage and computed cost. Held/undispatched results cannot pretend observed zero usage. Known usage may exceed its reservation: the client accepts the actual arithmetic rather than dropping it, while SQL must charge the full amount and block inconsistent/deficit admission. Unknown/cancel/crash holds cannot be released by a client fallback or clock expiry.

## Integer and identity contracts

Technical UUIDs/SHA values are canonical lowercase. Model/prompt/policy identifiers are 1–100 ASCII characters from the agreed identifier alphabet, without normalization. Requested/known usage counts are JavaScript-safe integers; reservations are positive and actual observed zero is valid. Receipt counters are canonical nonnegative decimal strings bounded to 128 digits, avoiding conversion of SQL aggregate values to floating point. Rates must be explicit positive integers within PostgreSQL's signed-bigint domain; no actual model prices are supplied by this module or handoff.

Cost uses one combined ceiling: `(input * inputRate + output * outputRate + 999999) / 1000000`, using BigInt throughout. A synthetic one-token input plus one-token output at synthetic rate one each costs one microUSD, not two separately rounded units. These are arithmetic vectors, not model-price claims. Logical identity uses SHA256 of `AIBUDGET1` plus LF and ten fixed-order UTF8 length-prefixed atoms: user, organization, operation, surface, payload SHA, model, prompt version, policy version and the two reserve counts. No JSON property-order hashing or candidate text is involved. The supplied neutral golden vector yields `aef2b4e2a2034dad016ec7d92116ef2e16c5f2f64751101e153943f7458b91fe`.
