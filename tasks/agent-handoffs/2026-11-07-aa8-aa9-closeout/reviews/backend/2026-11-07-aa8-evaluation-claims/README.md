# AA085/AA086 evidence cards and claim controls

Owned implementation: `app/lib/server/evaluation-claims.ts` and its unit tests. This is a local claim proposal and monitor simulator, with no flag, configuration, publication, deployment, environment or hosted-storage changes. STOP ANY DEPLOYMENT remains in force; GitHub work belongs to the coordinator.

The version-1 dataset/ruleset/model/evidence card binds its source, lock, actual locked report payload, evaluation result and calibration/policy hashes. It retains scope, segments, limitations, expiry, explicit monitor limits and previous immutable version. The five labels are exactly Regression-tested, Corpus-validated, Domain-validated, Vendor-observed and Outcome-calibrated.

`assessEvaluationClaim(card, actualLockedReport, asOf, authority?)` validates the current upstream report shape, including missing measurements and withheld statistics. Trusted in-process execution and review authorization must bind the exact card/report payload, source, lock, policy, scope and receipt hashes; input readiness flags alone never grant eligibility. Synthetic evidence supports only Regression-tested. A genuinely executed and reviewed regression may retain failed or underpowered numeric evaluation and still qualify for that narrow label. It does not gain representative validity.

Higher claims require actual upstream representative readiness, every required segment/metric gate, sample justification and a separately authorized independent human review. Named-vendor observation and prospective outcome-study evidence are additional requirements at their respective levels. Future authorized branches are implemented and tested; they are not permanently disabled and do not imply that a real corpus, reviewer, vendor test or prospective study occurred. Card eligibility never itself activates or publishes a claim.

`monitorEvaluationClaim(card, asOf, context)` reads complete trusted monitor signals for the exact card hash. Missing, throwing, nonfinite, stale or future signals stop and propose rollback. Expiry, revocation, failed evaluation, source/lock/report/policy drift and complaint/correction/latency/cost/fairness/error thresholds do the same. The previous version is returned as an immutable rollback target. Missing monitor limits fail validation. No monitor action changes a production flag.

Actual checks: 10 focused tests pass with Node.js 22.23.4; strict TypeScript, ESLint, Prettier and diff checks pass. Tests execute the actual annotation/partition/calibration/locked-evaluation pipeline rather than a readiness stub. They cover synthetic-only claims, actual underpowered numeric failure, trusted exact-payload authorization, all future labels, failed/missing segment gates, sample/reviewer/vendor/study controls, expiry/drift and every monitor failure. Initial tests rejected the new upstream statistics shape because its missingMeasurements/withheld fields were absent from the strict adapter; the adapter now matches the current report without relaxing metric gates or changing observations.

The pipeline fixtures are synthetic contract executions. Their deliberately illustrative thresholds do not approve a real performance floor, and the future authority branches are not external research results. Independent technical review and combined workflow evidence belong to the coordinator.
