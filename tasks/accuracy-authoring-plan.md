## 1. Outcome

Evolve Resumide from a transparent synthetic-regression analyzer into a candidate-first, evidence-grounded workspace that:

- reads native, scanned, and mixed resumes with measured confidence and candidate correction;
- reports deterministic ATS Compatibility and job-specific Job Alignment without pretending to know an employer's hidden decision;
- lets candidates fix exact lines on the result page using verified facts and reversible AI suggestions;
- produces versioned PDF/DOCX and later LaTeX artifacts;
- parses every export again before calling it verified;
- earns domain/vendor claims one gate at a time through representative evaluation.

## 2. Delivery constraints

## 15. Risks and mitigations

| Risk                                                | Impact   | Mitigation                                                                                        |
| --------------------------------------------------- | -------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A polished number exceeds evidence                  | Critical | Separate dimensions, confidence gate, claim ladder, locked evaluation, human approval             |
| OCR/provider confidence is miscalibrated            | High     | Provider bake-off, corpus calibration, disagreement preservation, candidate correction            |
| LLM inserts plausible false experience              | Critical | Fact IDs, candidate confirmation, deterministic verifier, accepted-change ledger                  |
| Keyword optimization becomes stuffing               | High     | Typed requirements, evidence strength, no duplicate credit, human-readable checks                 |
| Domain aggregate hides weak segments                | High     | Per-segment gates and lower confidence bounds                                                     |
| Real resume data creates privacy risk               | Critical | Permissioned acquisition, minimization, anonymization, isolation, retention/deletion              |
| PDF/DOCX/LaTeX looks correct but parses incorrectly | High     | Parse-back comparison and verified label policy                                                   |
| LaTeX compilation becomes an execution boundary     | Critical | Isolated compiler, allowlist, no network/shell escape, hard resource caps                         |
| Free-product AI cost grows                          | High     | Deterministic-first pipeline, bounded small-model path, escalation by uncertainty, cost benchmark |
| Employer mode creates discrimination/selection risk | Critical | Separate gate, protected-feature exclusion, adverse-impact review, notice, appeal, human decision |
| Legacy scores are silently reinterpreted            | High     | Versioned legacy adapter, non-comparable label, migration/read-model tests                        |
