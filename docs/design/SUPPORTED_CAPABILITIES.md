# Supported capabilities and claim boundaries

Version: `supported-capabilities/1.0.0`. Date: 2026-11-09. Status: **CR-C00 reviewed engineering contract; required repairs remain open**. This contract records the current advertised surfaces, their measured limits and the required repair targets. Documentation review can close CR-C00; it does not repair or release any capability below.

The [approved remediation plan](../../tasks/reliability-remediation-plan.md), [product specification](../ATS_SCORING_AND_AUTHORING_SPEC.md), [constraints](../../CONSTRAINTS.md) and accepted decisions remain governing requirements. The [consumer audit](../evaluation/CONSUMER_VALIDATION_20261109.md) is the current evidence baseline: 615 bound source inputs, 1,453 unit/integration tests, 65 browser passes, one unresolved consumer expectation and two preserved selector-harness failures. These counts describe engineering checks, not population accuracy or a universal product guarantee. Subsequent repairs need new source-bound evidence.

## Current mode and product boundaries

All current services, including AI and Build, remain free of commercial charges. Pricing implementation and the future plan proposal are deferred until the owner resumes them after testing and feedback. Free access does not remove technical resource, rate, concurrency, consent or provider-budget limits. A resource-limit response must not invent a paid upgrade requirement.

Deployment and publication remain on hold. This document authorizes no provider call, new credential, hosted schema change, real-data collection, Docker build, service start, commit or deployment. Supabase supplies database/auth/storage directly; Docker is for workers/compiler only. Preserve unrelated resources and existing dirty work.

The candidate analyzer and standalone Build workspace are separate:

- The consumer upload route accepts PDF input. Native-text, scanned and mixed PDFs belong to the core repair envelope; scanned and mixed support is currently incomplete, not removed from the target. A DOCX export is not evidence of DOCX ingestion. Broader document-engine/Accuracy-v2 DOCX objectives remain separate recorded work; no DOCX upload claim is introduced or silently canceled here.
- Canonical resume rendering exports PDF and DOCX from an exact persisted version, subject to confirmation, source/provenance and parse-back checks. It does not compile arbitrary LaTeX.
- The authenticated `/build` workspace accepts supported single-file LaTeX and provides code editing, visual PDF preview and PDF download. Under [ADR-0016](../decisions/0016-standalone-latex-converter.md), it does not call the analyzer, create canonical facts, award scores or automatically upload a generated PDF. The candidate may explicitly download and then submit that PDF to the analyzer with its separate consent.
- The approved first domain is software engineering and the validation segment is English / India-focused resumes under [ADR-0009](../decisions/0009-aa0-truth-contract-and-evaluation-design.md) and [the product decision register](../ATS_SCORING_AND_AUTHORING_SPEC.md#18-decision-register-after-aa0-approval). This is an engineering/evaluation target, not release support: the segment gates remain open. Other domains remain unsupported until separately validated. A parser accepting bytes or recognizing a term outside that domain does not establish a released occupation, language, locale or layout segment. Additional support needs a reviewed versioned capability and evidence; existing promised core repairs cannot be narrowed merely to hide failed cases.

## Frozen Build support envelope

The accepted [Build design](LATEX_CONVERTER.md) and [ADR-0016](../decisions/0016-standalone-latex-converter.md) define one complete article document, reviewed macro/layout forms and the versioned package/runtime closure. Current package roots are titlesec, latexsym, amssymb, amsmath, enumitem, geometry, hyperref and xcolor, with reviewed transitive files. Source settings choose the permitted page geometry. Keep the accepted source bytes and typography; no forced font/geometry rewrite is introduced here.

This is not unrestricted Overleaf/project compatibility. Other classes, arbitrary package options/programs, user-uploaded includes/fonts/images/bibliography, runtime package downloads, host access and shell escape are outside the accepted standalone profile unless separately reviewed. The compiler runs only in its admitted disposable worker with its enforced no-network/filesystem/resource policy; never fall back to host TeX or widen a closure to make a test pass. Limits remain those in the current code/design and approved runtime manifest. A compatibility rejection is actionable; a required supported macro/profile failing remains a repair defect rather than a new unsupported declaration.

## Trust, confirmation and default review

1. **Extracted candidate:** immutable native/OCR text and exact citations show what was observed. They do not establish a correct candidate fact, calibrated confidence or matching entity.
2. **Candidate-authored correction:** retain its event, actor authority and stable owner/source/attempt/entity/slot/revision binding. Do not attach an old citation that quotes a different value. Latest means within that target, never latest by field over the entire resume.
3. **Canonical confirmation:** imports/materialized corrections are unconfirmed until a separate exact-value/version action. Confirmation does not repair OCR accuracy, clear calibration, adopt a scoring policy or survive later changed text automatically. Existing versions use an explicit diff/CAS child; ambiguous targets require choice, missing facts require a reviewed add path, and replay/undo preserve lineage.
4. **AI proposal:** bounded supported wording or a question/advice state, never an accepted fact or score. Every mode must preserve actor/action/object, contribution, date/skill and metric value/unit/direction/scope support; a citation, valid JSON or shared number alone is insufficient. A reviewed formatting size is distinct from a business metric. Unsupported factual output cannot leak through Copy or Apply as grounded content.
5. **Checked artifact:** parse-back demonstrates fidelity to its admitted source/version under the renderer policy, not truth, employer acceptance or general document accessibility. Unconfirmed/unsupported facts and failed parity remain blocked under the current artifact contract.
6. **Unavailable/review/unsupported:** show the actual reason and safe recovery. Do not convert an unknown, failed parse, unadopted policy, absent field or unsupported segment into zero candidate quality or a promised numerical improvement.

Current evidence/2.0.0 attempts remain uncalibrated and scoring-ineligible; candidate review does not promote them to verified/high confidence. Future high/verified evidence states or authoritative values require the existing active-policy/admission and empirical gates. Measured diagnostic counts may still be displayed with their meaning and source; they are not a general ATS percentage.

Each acceptance receipt binds actual flags, source/producer/schema/policy/model versions and runtime image where relevant. Recheck ownership, membership, active source, consent, deletion/revocation, exact version and concurrency at the server boundary before dispatch/apply/publication. A client-visible plan, hash, confirmation checkbox, connection ID or success redirect is not authority by itself.

Keep candidate text, uploaded source/PDF, prompts/responses, credentials, tokens and cookies out of source, logs, telemetry and public evidence. Build source/PDF remain transient under their existing contract. Preserve private storage, retention/deletion and exact synthetic cleanup; new correction/provenance or auth records must participate in them. Current free usage still obeys technical limits; rejected output, cancellation or unknown provider usage must not become invented zero cost or free capacity.
