## Purpose and invariants

Qualitative reviews, explicit rewrites and Build assistance must share one organization budget for each UTC calendar month. Separate feature counters cannot enforce that pool. Tokens and integer cost units are technical safety limits; they are not customer charges or a promise of unlimited AI use. Editor, compile, preview and PDF download do not consume an AI reservation.

The database chooses the period from its own clock. Each attempt keeps its original period through dispatch, late completion and reconciliation. The first admitted policy freezes the pool's monthly token/cost limits. A new operation may select a different reviewed model/price policy while using the same pool; rotation cannot create another allowance or replace those limits. The operation captures its model, prompt policy, pricing rates and permitted attempt count once. Limits and positive integer price units come from a reviewed database policy, never arbitrary reservation arguments. Missing or unapproved prices deny admission; zero is not a substitute for unknown pricing.

Cost is one combined integer ceiling: `ceil((inputTokens * inputMicrousdPerMillion + outputTokens * outputMicrousdPerMillion) / 1000000)`. Use the captured rates for both reservation and settlement. Decimal-string counters cross the TypeScript/SQL boundary without floating-point currency arithmetic or invented zero usage. Overflow must fail closed while preserving the pending hold, never wrap or erase observed spend.

Each logical operation binds its organization, current owner, feature, payload hash, model and prompt/policy identity. Every provider dispatch also has a unique attempt identity and policy-bounded ordinal; the permitted one-to-three attempts do not create an automatic retry. Exact replay returns its existing state without authorizing another dispatch; a conflicting identity fails. Reservation and dispatch-claim replay check current organization ownership and membership before lookup. Revocation denies new AI permission. The server can still settle or mark unknown an exactly bound, already dispatched attempt after membership or consent revocation; otherwise known spend would be discarded. That accounting authority does not permit another dispatch or expose a consumer lookup endpoint. Administrative reconciliation has a separate operator allowlist.

Reservation, dispatch claim and settlement are database transactions under a consistent lock order. Only the first successful dispatch claim authorizes one provider request. Reserve before making that request. Release is legal only when no dispatch occurred. A timeout, cancellation, worker crash or unknown usage after dispatch keeps the conservative reservation pending without automatic expiry. Provider refusal, malformed output and grounding rejection still cost tokens when usage is known. Settle that usage even if result persistence fails.

Known usage above the input, output or cost reservation is charged in full and blocks new admission even when the month still has capacity. It is not discarded or clamped to the reserve. A separately allowlisted operator may acknowledge an already settled overrun using identical observed usage, a reconciliation ID and evidence hash. That appends one audit event without charging again or lowering usage. It can clear the reviewed attempt's overrun fence; an actual monthly deficit remains blocked independently. Unknown-attempt reconciliation charges its known usage once and records that operator review. Replays must not debit twice. Monthly rollover must not reassign a late charge or erase an unresolved deficit.

The migration creates seven private tables: policies, period openings, pools, logical operations, attempts, events and organization-scoped reconciliation operators. None is seeded. Before first admission in a month, a reviewed opening record must name the pool policy, known opening spend and evidence hash, with zero unresolved legacy attempts. Missing or unknown opening accounting denies admission; the ledger never invents an automatic zero balance. Policies, openings, operations and events reject update/delete. Pool and attempt identity fields are immutable; only their accounting projections transition through the service RPCs.

## Failure and rollback

On admission, settlement, ownership or connectivity uncertainty, stop new AI dispatch and keep the deterministic/partial result path available. A client abort is not a refund. Do not reset a month, delete the ledger, change the bound policy or expire holds to regain capacity. Reconcile pending attempts from approved provider evidence through append-only receipts. If safe reconciliation is unavailable, retain the hold and report the unavailable AI state.

Rollback disables new AI admission and restores the last compatible application artifact while preserving the forward schema and all attempt/reconciliation records. Do not use a down migration, truncate or reseed to restore capacity. Resume only after the exact policy, producer cutover, retained balances and ownership checks are verified. Source/unit review does not authorize a hosted migration, live spend, deployment or release.
