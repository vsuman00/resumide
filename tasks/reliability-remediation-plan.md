## 1. Outcome and boundaries

Deliver reliable supported consumer journeys from upload and JD to truthful recommendations, candidate-approved edits and verified exports; make standalone Build code editing, AI assistance and LaTeX-to-PDF conversion work within their declared limits. Every supported positive case must succeed; uncertain, unsafe and unsupported cases must produce correct actionable states without invented facts or numbers. A warning, hidden button, mock response or default-off flag alone does not repair a promised capability. No finite test suite can guarantee every possible document or employer ATS; the product must state its support envelope and detect when it is outside it.

The owner approved implementation and validation after reviewing this plan. Implement bounded repairs and their checks; a plan approval does not itself authorize new credentials/provider scope, hosted DDL/target, real-data collection, publication or deployment. Prepare concrete reviewed side-effect packets where needed. All services remain free of commercial charges; the future INR 300/month and monthly free allowance of 10 reviews/one edit proposal and any paid Build gate stay deferred until the owner resumes pricing after testing and consumer feedback. Keep operational abuse/spend limits. Supabase supplies database/auth/storage directly; Docker is worker/compiler only, never the database. Preserve unrelated Evoloryx resources and dirty work.

Preserve the modular monolith, source/version provenance, server ownership/consent/CAS, leased workers, default-off production controls, old schema readers and forward migrations. Deterministic rules own numbers; AI cannot invent facts, award points or silently change accepted content. Existing numerical floors, failed locked receipts and [claim limits](../docs/ATS_SCORING_AND_AUTHORING_SPEC.md) remain. Sole owner plus AI technical review is the engineering workflow; no reviewer team is required now. Stronger representative/domain/vendor claims still need the separately deferred evidence.

## 3. Contracts to freeze before dependent code

**Evidence and corrections.** Distinguish contact slots from employment/education/certification entities. Multiple employers or dates are not automatically contradictions. A stable target binds owner, source hash/version, evidence attempt, entity/assertion slot and revision. Prefer derived grouping over rewriting immutable evidence. Any changed stored envelope must receive a versioned producer/admission/replay contract and historical reader before new writes.

A correction is candidate-authored provenance referencing its event and target; it never receives an old citation quoting a different value or becomes calibrated source truth. Use the latest applicable event within that exact target, never the latest value for a field across the whole resume. Initial import carries the corrected value as unconfirmed; canonical confirmation is a separate exact-value/version transition. Existing canonical edits require a visible diff and CAS child, not overwrite. Ambiguous targets require a choice; absent facts need an explicit reviewed add/clarification path. Replays do not duplicate facts. Restore/undo creates a new version and retains the complete lineage. The normal corrected-value journey requires no repeated manual entry.

**Factual AI output.** Build a bounded intermediate representation of fact IDs, actor/action/object, contribution/entity, dates, skill evidence and metric value/unit/baseline/direction/scope. Every proposed assertion must be a supported transformation of those relationships or an explicitly candidate-confirmed new declaration. Digits found elsewhere, known words, valid JSON, a citation or an LLM's assurance are insufficient. Swapping metrics between projects, changing worked-on to led, negation, increased/reduced direction and causal scope must fail. Unknown support produces a clarification question or rejection, never grounded prose. Preserve useful supported wording and manual editing. [OpenAI's schema guidance](https://developers.openai.com/api/docs/guides/structured-outputs) explicitly allows model mistakes; schema validation is one layer.

Use the same support contract for initial qualitative text, legacy drafts/copy, explicit rewrites, canonical proposals and every Build mode. Fix mode may alter approved LaTeX syntax while preserving candidate text; content-changing Rewrite cannot escape fact checks; Resume mode requires evidence and exact confirmation. Formatting numbers such as font sizes are not candidate business metrics. Review/apply binds the exact source/selection/policy and resets consent/confirmation when those change. AI output never directly writes a score or accepted resume.

**JD and scores.** Keep the source wording, such as JS/Postgres, in the grounded value/quotation; store normalized concepts separately with a versioned mapping. Typed OR groups and lower/upper bounds must survive SQL/RPC admission and replay, not only TypeScript. Preserve unfamiliar explicit requirement clauses as unmapped/review-required rather than omitting them. Separate required, preferred, contextual, unknown and negated requirements. Company/benefit/revenue text is not candidate evidence. A skill mention is not proof of proficiency or years.

Compatibility, job alignment, writing clarity and factual truth remain distinct. Before/after deltas come from the same deterministic versioned policy/input pair, never a model promise. No credit from stuffing, identity changes or silently excluding unknown requirements. A15 can close engineering admission/explainability while a numerical policy remains unadopted. G02 is the later empirical-adoption boundary; until then preserve withholding and useful non-numeric diagnostics. Do not make confirmation alone stand in for extraction calibration or population validity.

**Runtime and budgets.** Treat commercial pricing and technical resource admission separately. Use durable payload-bound operations, atomic reserve/settle/reconcile, explicit attempts and lease fences. Known pre-dispatch failure may release a reservation; after dispatch, rejected output, cancellation or unknown usage cannot automatically become zero cost/free capacity. Retry 401/403 zero times; bound transient retries/Retry-After/deadlines within approved budgets. Require one server-derived source/JD/policy binding at every final write. A stale worker cannot overwrite a newer version or complete after lease loss.

**Compiler and OCR.** Resolve the compiler transport through verified source artifacts and hashes, not unpinned replacements, removed security checks or package deletion to lower a scan score. A controlled verified build context/named input is a candidate design, with provenance, licensing and image admission updated consistently; [Docker supports checksum validation](https://docs.docker.com/reference/dockerfile/#add---checksum) and [named build contexts](https://docs.docker.com/build/concepts/context/#named-contexts). A reproducible build, a secure runtime and a visually/semantically correct PDF are separate checks. Known absence can fail before create; ambiguous/late container creation retains quarantine and exact cleanup. No network, host mounts, shell escape or unbounded resources.

The production OCR adapter must be explicit and policy/version/owner-bound, not a production reuse of a parameter labelled syntheticOcr. Preserve native evidence on mixed documents. OCR confidence cannot certify itself. Repair/tune development data only; original block-font failures stay failed until their original assigned gates pass. Declaring that segment unsupported may limit the product claim but does not erase the failed gate or complete promised support.
