## Actual implementation

`evaluateRequirementCoverage(rawContext: unknown)` returns the strict `RequirementCoverageSchema`, version `requirement-coverage/1.0.0`. It validates the actual owned context and derives the real semantic producer internally. There is no accepted caller witness array, scoring permission or approval flag. The producer revalidates its context through the shared decoder; these are bounded local passes, not additional database reads. Failure returns a fixed unavailable branch without raw errors or private rows.

Binding retains the exact source, attempt, canonical/JD version and revision, opaque native content hashes, reader profiles and semantic/duration/coverage policy identities. The result has a deterministic digest. The matcher does not compare native PostgreSQL hashes with JavaScript serialization hashes.

Actual persisted SHA clause IDs, source quotes, requirement UUIDs, importance, polarity, duration bounds and retained unmapped clauses remain intact. Empty operand/requirement collections are preserved. AND/OR aggregate explicit operands; negated, unknown-importance and contextual clauses do not become positive qualifications. Actual operand and clause states remain `not_evaluated` under the uncalibrated policy. Proposed states use the frozen MatchState names and fixed missing-evidence explanations.

Matching uses an approved concept identity or ASCII case/space exact literal identity, without inferred neighboring skills. Aliases share a subject group. Repeated evidence groups select one representative; repeated clauses/operands retain their original rows and identify prior semantic duplicates. Required/preferred importance stays separate. No weight, numerical score or qualification probability is computed.

Exact cited contributions with whole-current-fact owner confirmation may support a bounded literal experience observation. Listed skills and uncited responsibility/date declarations alone remain unknown with the recorded declaration acknowledged; they do not ask the owner to confirm the same value again or independently establish proficiency. Explicit proficiency clauses remain partial even with a confirmed contribution. Unconfirmed exact source contributions retain their specific confirmation need.

Duration calculations use every distinct matching interval for that same skill subject, rather than the displayed references. A separate result uses only whole-confirmed source intervals. Reconstructed source clauses have null whole-clause confirmation: confirmation of the anchor/date fragment is never promoted to confirmation of the full dated claim. Missing/ambiguous/outside-bound periods preserve their limited interpretation. The duration utility's 256-interval cap fails closed; no partial interval sum or hidden truncation is used.

Each operand displays at most 20 witness and fact references and reports additional counts. Global counts distinguish compatible witnesses, evidence-group representatives, displayed references, additional matching representatives and unmatched witnesses. Only displayed witness records are copied; their complete source/declaration/offset/supporting-fact records remain intact. All semantic unresolved records and original retained JD clauses remain visible. Aggregate work, operand/clause and output-byte limits fail closed.
